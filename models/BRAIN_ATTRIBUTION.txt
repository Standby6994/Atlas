Original upstream notices are preserved unchanged. Their University of Washington credit is incomplete or mistaken according to the provenance discussion at https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy/issues/7 . That issue identifies the telencephalon white-matter boundary pair with Brain for Blender; this is an attribution inference, not a new geometry comparison performed for this atlas.

If this pair is omitted by the viewer, canonical IDs are White matter of telencephalon.l and White matter of telencephalon.r. This supplement does not assert Brain for Blender provenance for spinal-cord white matter.
